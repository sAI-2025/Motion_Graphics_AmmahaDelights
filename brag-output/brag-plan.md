# Brag Plan: AmAha Delights

## What is this app?
AmAha Delights is a Hyderabad-based premium sweet-treats brand (soft-serve "Softy", Belgian waffles, handcrafted Coconut Delights / Kobbari Munju) that also sells smart vending machines (softy, popcorn, coke) for offices, malls and events.

## The angle
A warm, appetite-first premium product film. The premise: the same brand that puts a cone of softy in your hand also machines it 24/7. Show the treats as hero objects, then reveal the machines as the way the delight scales. Cream canvas, gold-to-caramel warmth, serif display type — the site's real identity, pushed to video intensity.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
Full-bleed hero dessert shot, warm cream gradient rising, and the site's own headline landing big: **"Delight in Every Bite."** No setup, no logo — just the craving.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Three signature treats fan in one at a time as real product photography — Premium Softy, Belgian Waffles, Coconut Delights — each with its real badge ("Popular" / "New").
- The pivot to machines: a real vending-machine panel slides in while three machine rows tick on with checkmarks (Softy, Popcorn, Coke), each row a real feature line from the site.
- The "24/7" badge riding the cool accent hue against the warm field.

## Outro / punchline
The wordmark assembles — **AmAha Delights** — over "Delight in Every Bite", then the real contact line: Swaraj Nagar, Hyderabad, and "Get a Quote". Warm bell payoff, music fades.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only (marketing site, no in-app flow). Centerpiece is the real product photography plus a recreated vending-machine "feature panel" built from the site's own machine copy.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: warm, appetite-driven premium dessert brand film — sun-lit, craving-first, confident but joyful
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, soft crossfades, restrained motion; let the product photography carry; no jokes, no stat-bragging, no chaos.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#FCFBF7` (hsl 40 50% 98%) — warm cream
- Accent (warm): gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #F5CB4C 0%, #C17E5C 100%)` (gold → caramel)
- Accent (cool): `#98D2E2` (hsl 193 56% 74%)
- Text: `#533C2D` (hsl 24 30% 25%) warm ink; muted `#9C8A7C`
- Display font: Playfair Display (900 / 700, italic for the mark)
- Body font: Poppins (400 / 500 / 700)
- Strongest visual element: the hero dessert photograph, the three product cards with their "Popular"/"New" badges, and the softy vending machine

## Share copy (draft)
AmAha Delights — creamy softy, Belgian waffles, and Kobbari Munju, now with smart vending machines that serve them 24/7. Delight in every bite.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm professional bed with a few tasteful, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady, clean — polished)
- Music treatment: fade in over 0.8s; sit under the whole film; fade out across 18.3–19.9s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (vol-12, ~109.96 BPM). Strong cues in window: 8.74s, 10.93s, 13.11s, 17.47s, 18.56s. Lock the logo payoff to ~17.47s and the machine-rows reveal to 10.93s / 13.11s. Beat grid used for the treat-card sequence.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; male music RMS lifts a warm glow behind the hero and gives the vending panel a soft presence. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint (low/medium HF-risk picks)
- Audio-coupled moments: treat cards arriving one by one (card slides), machine rows ticking on (soft clicks), logo payoff (warm bell)
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no comedic cues; nothing competes with the headline read

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: "Delight in Every Bite" — 4.0s
Full-bleed `hero-desserts.jpg` with a slow ken-burns (scale 1.06 → 1.0). Cream gradient rises from the bottom (the site's `--gradient-overlay`). Centered stack: a glass eyebrow pill "Premium Sweet Treats" with a sparkle glyph; the headline in Playfair 900 — "Delight in" in the warm gradient, "Every Bite" in ink; a one-line subhead from the real hero copy. Warm radial glow breathes with music RMS behind the type.
Sequential/interaction: yes — eyebrow, then headline line 1, then line 2, then subline arrive in sequence.
Audio intent: warm, inviting; a single soft drop announces the eyebrow, a soft thud lands the headline.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop for the eyebrow pop; soft reveal hit when the headline settles.
Music: warm upbeat bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Signature Treats — 6.0s
Cream field with two soft radial glows (warm + pink), a huge ghost word "DELIGHT" at low opacity drifting, and a hairline rule under a section header: "Our Signature **Delights**". Three product cards fan in one by one: Premium Softy (sunday.png, badge "Popular"), Belgian Waffles (waffle.png, badge "New"), Coconut Delights (coconut-product.png) — image, title, and the site's real one-liner each. Cards hold fully visible after the third arrives.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards arrive one at a time, left to right.
Audio intent: light, tactile card arrivals.
Audio-coupled idea: card-slide SFX on each card; hold the full set on screen after the third.
Music: same bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Smart Vending — 5.5s
Cream field shifts cooler: a soft blue radial glow and a ghost "24/7". Left: a large rounded panel with `softy-machine.jpg` and a "24/7 Operation" badge. Right: header "Smart Vending Solutions" + three machine rows (Softy Vending Machine, Popcorn Vending Machine, Coke Vending Machine) with checkmarks and a real feature line each, arriving in sequence on the beat grid.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three rows tick on one at a time.
Audio intent: decisive, building; a warm bell opens the panel, soft clicks tick the rows.
Audio-coupled idea: bong on the panel reveal; click per row; lock row reveals to strong cues 10.93s / 13.11s.
Music: same bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro / Wordmark — 4.5s
Warm gradient flourish blooms from center; grain and a soft vignette. The wordmark **AmAha Delights** assembles in Playfair (gradient warm), above the italic tagline "Delight in Every Bite", the real contact line "Swaraj Nagar · Hyderabad, India", and a CTA chip "Get a Quote". Logo payoff locked to the strong cue at ~17.47s. Long hold to the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — wordmark, then tagline, then contact/CTA.
Audio intent: warm resolution; one resonant bell on the wordmark.
Audio-coupled idea: bell at the wordmark landing; music fades under the final hold.
Music: warm bed, fading
Transition mood: none — hold to end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, upbeat, business-clean (polished)
